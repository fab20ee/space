A box of portable meters for the jobs where the bench multimeter is in the wrong place: checking
a battery in your bag, testing continuity on a cable across the room, or holding a meter on a
board in the laser cutter while someone else watches the display.

The BSIDE S10 is a "smart" meter: touch the probes to something and it decides on its own whether
that is voltage, resistance or continuity, and picks the range. It also has a non-contact voltage
detector for finding live wires. The second meter is a plain manual-range multimeter — less
convenient, but nothing to go wrong, and fine for teaching.

## What they are good for

- Quick checks of batteries, adapters and supplies
- Continuity and short-circuit tests on cables and boards
- Measuring resistors, checking diodes and LEDs
- Finding live conductors with the NCV detector (S10)
- Lending to a second person while the bench meter is in use

## Safety

- These are CAT II/III hand-held meters, not clamp meters — do not measure current in mains
  circuits with them.
- On the manual meter, set the function and range before touching the circuit. Voltage on the
  current range blows the fuse or worse.
- Keep fingers behind the probe guards.
- Do not trust NCV as proof that a wire is dead. It shows that it is live; the opposite needs a
  proper measurement.

## How to use

1. Take a meter and the leads out of the box; put them back in the same box when done.
2. S10: switch on, touch the probes to the circuit, read. Use the mode button to force a function
   if the auto mode guesses wrong. Charge it over USB when the battery indicator says so.
3. Manual meter: select the function and range with the dial, then measure.
4. If a reading looks wrong, check the leads with a continuity test on themselves first.

## Access

The meters are in the hackspace room, so a plan that includes hackspace access is required. They
stay on site — do not take them home.
