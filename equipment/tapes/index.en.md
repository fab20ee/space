The box of sticky things. Nothing complicated, but when you need it you need it now, and there is
no time to search the hackspace.

## Which is which

Choosing a tape is not a matter of taste; each has its job:

- **PVC insulating tape** — insulating electrical joints and colour coding. It stretches, conforms
  and stays on a wire. Over time it creeps and leaves adhesive, so for a permanent joint use
  [heat shrink](/en/equipment/wires-connectors-and-crimping) instead.
- **Masking (paper) tape** — anything that has to come off cleanly: masking for paint, holding a
  part temporarily, or writing on it with a marker as a label.
- **Packing tape** — boxes, parcels, bulk. No use at all for electrical work.
- **Double-sided** — sticking a board or a panel where you cannot drill.
- **Reinforced** — rough fixing, temporary repairs, things that have to hold properly.

## A couple of things that save grief

- **Insulating tape is not insulation.** At mains voltage, tape wound round a joint is not a
  repair, it is a postponed problem.
- **Masking tape has to come off in time.** After a week in the sun it bakes on and starts coming
  away in layers.
- **Do not stick tape to circuit boards or optics.** The adhesive stays, and getting it off takes
  alcohol and patience.
- Fold the end of the roll back on itself. The next person will thank you for not having to
  scratch at the roll with a fingernail for half an hour.
