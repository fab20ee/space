The chair is what you actually sit on for eight hours, so these are not "visitor chairs" but
full-size work chairs: a breathable mesh back, an adjustable headrest and flip-up armrests. The
mesh supports your back without turning August into a sauna.

A couple of desks also have a tall drafting chair with a foot ring — if you like sitting high at
a raised desk, it is the middle mode between sitting and standing.

## Adjustments

- Seat height — gas lift, lever under the seat
- Backrest tilt with a lock
- Headrest height and angle
- Armrests flip up, so the chair slides completely under the desktop

## Access

There is a chair at every desk, included in every plan. Adjust it to yourself without hesitation —
the next person will readjust it anyway.
