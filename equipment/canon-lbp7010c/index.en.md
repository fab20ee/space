A laser printer for documents: text and diagrams come out sharp and do not smudge, the sheet is dry
at once. Colour is there but slow, four pages a minute; for photos the Epson inkjet next to it is
better.

## How to use

1. Print from the computer that stands next to the printers; the printer is already set up there.
2. Paper is on the shelf above the printers: plain, self-adhesive, film, photo paper. Put only
   laser-rated paper in the laser printer; inkjet film and photo paper melt in it.
3. Printing and paper within reason are included in the price.

## Access

In the coworking room, available with any plan.
