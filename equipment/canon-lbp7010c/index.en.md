A compact colour laser for the things paper is still better at: contracts to sign, a printed
draft to read on the sofa, labels for the boxes in the workshop. Laser toner does not smudge and
does not run in the rain, so this is the printer for anything that leaves the building.

Colour pages are slow — four a minute — so for a thick colour report use the Epson instead, and
keep this one for text and the occasional colour page.

## What it is good for

- Documents, contracts and forms in black and white
- Labels, tags and signs on thick paper or card
- A colour page or two when the quality matters more than speed

## How to use

1. Send the job from the coworking computer, or ask how to add the printer to your laptop.
2. Choose A4, single or double-sided by hand — the printer has no automatic duplex.
3. For card or labels, use the manual feed and select the paper type in the driver so the fuser
   runs hotter.
4. Take your pages and any misprints with you; recycle them in the paper bin.
5. If the toner warning shows or the paper jams, tell us rather than fighting the machine.

## Access

The printer is in the coworking room and is included in every plan — day, week and month.
Ordinary use is free; if you print a book, ask first.
