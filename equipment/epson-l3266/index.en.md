The inkjet for colour: photos, stickers, posters for events, plus a scanner and copying from the
lid. Ink lives in tanks, not cartridges, so colour printing is not rationed. For a stack of
black-and-white documents the Canon laser next to it is faster.

## How to use

1. Print from the computer that stands next to the printers; the printer is already set up there.
2. Paper is on the shelf above the printers: plain, self-adhesive, film, photo paper. Photo paper
   goes glossy side towards you into the rear tray, one or two sheets at a time.
3. Scanning: put the sheet on the glass, start the scan from the computer or with the button on the
   printer. A copy is made with a button, no computer needed.
4. Printing and paper within reason are included in the price.

## Access

In the coworking room, available with any plan.
