The all-rounder of the coworking room: it prints in colour, scans, and copies without a computer.
Because it is an EcoTank the ink comes from refillable tanks rather than cartridges, so colour
pages cost almost nothing and nobody minds you printing a poster draft in full colour.

The scanner is the part most people come for — sign a contract, scan it to PDF, email it, done.
It works from your phone over Wi-Fi as well as from a laptop.

## What it is good for

- Colour prints: presentations, mock-ups, photos on photo paper
- Scanning signed documents, receipts and drawings to PDF or JPEG
- Quick copies straight from the panel, no computer needed
- Printing from a phone or tablet over Wi-Fi

## How to use

1. **Print:** connect to the printer over Wi-Fi (or ask for the network) and print from any
   device. On a phone, the Epson Smart Panel app or the built-in print dialogue both work.
2. **Scan:** lift the lid, put the page face down against the corner mark, and start the scan
   from the panel to a USB stick, or from your device with the app.
3. **Copy:** put the original on the glass, choose colour or mono on the panel, press Start.
4. Photo paper and thick paper go in the rear tray one sheet at a time.
5. Take your originals off the glass and close the lid when you are done.

## Access

The printer is in the coworking room and is included in every plan — day, week and month.
Ordinary use is free; if you are printing hundreds of colour pages, ask first.
