A microscope for soldering and inspection: under it you can see missed and bridged joints on
fine-pitch leads, the marking on resistors the size of a sesame seed, cracks in traces. The screen
is on the stand, so you solder right under the lens while looking at the screen, not into an
eyepiece. Photos and video go to a microSD card, which is inserted. There is an HDMI output, but it
is not connected to anything.

## How to use

1. Check that the microscope is plugged into USB power and switched on with the button on the
   power cable. The button on the unit alone does not switch it on; that is not obvious.
2. Put the board on the stage, move the head up or down the stand until the image is sharp. Fine
   focus is the ring on the lens.
3. The IR remote lies next to it: it controls the brightness. If the spot you need is washed out or
   in shadow, fix the brightness first, then move the lamps on their flexible arms.
4. The button on the body takes a photo; a long press or the separate button records video. Files
   are on the microSD card, which can be taken out and copied to a computer.
5. When done, switch off with the button on the cable and put the remote back.

## Take care

- Do not solder right under the lens at minimum distance: flux fumes settle on the glass. Clean the
  lens only with an optics cloth.
- Move the head along the stand gently; the stand is plastic.

## Access

The microscope is on the electronics bench in the hackspace room, so a plan with hackspace access
is required. No induction is needed. The microscope stays in the room.
