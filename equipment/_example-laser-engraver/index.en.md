## Safety

- Always run the air assist and exhaust before starting a job.
- Never leave the machine unattended while it is running.
- PVC and vinyl release toxic fumes — never cut them.

## How to use

1. Prepare your file in LightBurn or Inkscape.
2. Place your material on the bed and focus the head.
3. Run a small test pass on a scrap of the same material.
4. Start the job and stay nearby.
