The projector for meetings, presentations and screenings in the coworking room. The resolution is
WUXGA, 1920 × 1200 — slightly taller than ordinary Full HD, and those extra 120 lines show up
exactly where they are wanted: in a table, in code on screen, in a slide with small text.

Three thousand lumens is enough that you do not have to turn the lights fully off. The picture
will be paler in a lit room, but readable.

## What it is good for

- Meetings and presentations in the coworking room
- Talks and screenings at events
- Putting code or a schematic on the wall when there are more than three people round the table
- A film in the evening, if the wall suits
- Taking along to an event elsewhere

## Connecting it

1. **HDMI** is the main input, and there are two. A cable is provided; a USB-C laptop needs an
   adapter, and that one is yours.
2. Switch the projector on and pick the source with **Source Search** if it has not found it
   itself.
3. Set focus and size with **the rings on the lens**: the near one is focus, the far one zoom.
4. Keystone correction is in the menu, but it is better to stand the projector square: digital
   correction eats sharpness.
5. The built-in speaker is weak. For anything beyond a voice in a quiet room, bring external
   speakers.

## About the lamp

This projector has a lamp, not an LED, and it is consumed: 5000 hours, up to ten thousand in eco
mode.

- **Do not pull the plug out of a running projector.** After switch-off the fan has to cool the
  lamp, which takes a minute or two. A hot lamp with no airflow lives noticeably less.
- Do not block the vents and do not stand the projector on anything soft.
- If a lamp-life message appears on screen, tell us rather than ignoring it.

## Renting it

The projector goes out to events and home. Take it with its power lead and the HDMI cable; an
adapter for your own laptop is your own. The daily rate and the term are in the rental block on
this page.

## Access

The projector is in the coworking room, so a plan with coworking access is enough. No induction
is needed.
