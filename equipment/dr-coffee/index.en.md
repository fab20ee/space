Coffee is included in the price: tap the screen, get your cup. Beans and milk are shared; we clean
the machine once a day.

## How to use

1. Put a cup down, pick a drink on the screen.
2. If the beans or the milk run out, they are in the drawer under the machine: beans go into the
   hopper on top, milk goes into the mini fridge next to it and connects with the tube.
3. If the machine asks to empty the drip tray or the grounds container, do it; they pull out at the
   front.
4. Cups go into the dishwasher afterwards.

## Access

In the coworking room, available with any plan.
