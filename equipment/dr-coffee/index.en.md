An automatic bean-to-cup machine with a milk system — the reason "coffee included" here means a
cappuccino, not a sachet of instant. Pick a drink on the touchscreen and the machine grinds,
brews and froths the milk by itself.

The milk lives in the mini fridge next to the machine and is plumbed in directly — for a
cappuccino or a flat white there is nothing to pour anywhere. A kettle, tea and a microwave are
a couple of steps away in the kitchen.

## The menu

Espresso, coffee crema, americano, caffe latte, cappuccino, flat white, milk foam on its own,
and plain hot water — if you are a tea person.

## How to use it

1. Put a cup on the grate, pick a drink on the screen — that is all.
2. If the machine asks for water or beans, or complains about the drip tray — feel free to top
   it up and empty it yourself, or just drop us a message.

## Access

It stands in the coworking room and is included in every plan. Nobody counts your cups.
