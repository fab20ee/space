Not a machine, but you will need it with every machine on the electronics bench. The organiser
boxes hold the small parts that every project runs out of at 11 pm: resistors and capacitors in
labelled compartments, LEDs, diodes, jumper wires, a sensor kit for Arduino-style boards, and
the soldering consumables — solder, flux, desoldering wick, heat-shrink.

Consumables are included in the price within reason. Taking a handful of resistors and a metre
of solder for your project is what the boxes are for; filling a bag to take home is not.

## What is there

- Resistor and capacitor assortment kits, sorted by value
- LEDs, diodes, transistors, common ICs
- Sensor and module kits (temperature, distance, motion, displays)
- Arduino-compatible boards, breadboards and jumper wires
- Solder (mostly lead-free), flux pens and paste, desoldering wick
- Heat-shrink tubing, hook-up wire, tip cleaner

## How to use it well

1. Take what you need for the job at hand, not for the next three.
2. Put the box back where it was, and the lid on. A component kit with the compartments spilled
   is useless to everyone.
3. Leftover solder and wire go back in the box, not in the bin.
4. If a compartment is empty or a kit is used up, write it on the board or tell us — we buy what
   people actually use.
5. Need something specific and expensive? Ask before assuming it is free.

## Access

The boxes are on the electronics bench in the hackspace room, so a plan that includes hackspace
access is required.
