A big screen for presentations, going through a project or a schematic together, and movies at
events. It stands on a wheeled stand: roll it to the table you are sitting at.

## How to use

1. Switch on with the remote; it lies next to the screen.
2. Plug the free end of the HDMI or USB-C cable into your laptop; the cables are already connected
   to the screen.
3. Pick the input with the remote if the picture does not appear by itself.
4. Afterwards switch the screen off and leave the cables where they are.

## Take care

- Roll the stand slowly and by the stand, not by the screen. Unplug the power cord first.

## Access

In the coworking room, available with any plan.
