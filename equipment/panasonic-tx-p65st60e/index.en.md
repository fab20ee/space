A big screen on a rolling stand. Plug a laptop in over HDMI and the whole room can see your
slides, your code, or the film. It is a plasma panel, which means deep blacks and wide viewing
angles — good for a group sitting around it — and a warm-up time of a second or two.

It lives in the coworking room and gets used for presentations, workshops, remote calls where
several people are in the room, and the occasional evening film or game night.

## What it is good for

- Presentations and workshops for a group
- Video calls with several people on this side
- Pair programming or design reviews on a screen everyone can see
- Board game rules on the big screen, film nights, streaming a match

## Care

- Move the stand slowly and with both hands; the panel is heavy and top-heavy.
- Plasma panels can retain a static image if it is left for many hours — do not leave a
  paused slide or a logo on screen overnight.
- Do not touch the panel surface; dust it with a dry microfibre cloth.
- Switch it off when you leave. It draws real power.

## How to use

1. Switch on with the remote or the button on the panel.
2. Connect your laptop with the HDMI cable and select the matching HDMI input with the remote.
3. Set your laptop to mirror or extend the display; 1920 × 1080 is the native resolution.
4. Sound comes out of the built-in speakers; adjust volume on the remote.
5. When done, unplug your cable, put the remote back, and switch the screen off.

## Access

The screen is in the coworking room and is included in every plan. For a booked event, mention
that you need it when you reserve the room.
