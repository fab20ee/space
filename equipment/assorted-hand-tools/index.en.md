The shelf of specialist hand tools: everything smaller and finer than what is in the
[kit boxes](/en/equipment/hand-tool-kits).

You need it when the work has gone down to a scale where an ordinary screwdriver will not fit and
fingers will not do: opening an instrument, undoing a screw in a recessed hole, placing an SMD
part, removing a circlip.

## What is here

- **Precision screwdrivers** — slim, with a spinning cap for your fingertip. Some have insulated
  handles, for working near voltage.
- **Bit sets** of several dozen: besides ordinary cross and flat, there are Torx, Pentalobe,
  Tri-wing and the rest of what phones and laptops are closed with. The handles are magnetic, so
  bits hold themselves.
- **Tweezers**, and plenty of them: straight and bent, very fine tips for small SMD, flat ones for
  films, black ESD ones for boards, and reverse-action ones that hold a part until you squeeze
  them.
- **Small spanners and hex keys** — for the screws that turn up in instruments and mechanisms.

## Which to take

- **For SMD** — a fine tip and a light pair. An 0402 part is picked up with the very tips, not
  with the whole jaw.
- **For live boards** — the black ESD ones. An ordinary steel pair will short something it should
  not, first time.
- **Reverse-action** — for holding something for a long time without tiring: soldering, gluing,
  fitting small springs.
- **Torx and Pentalobe bits** are needed more often than you would think. Undoing a screw with the
  wrong bit means rounding out the head and then drilling it out.

## How to use

1. Take the tool, work, put it back. This shelf only works that way.
2. Return bits to their own slot in the set, not to a pocket and not to a heap.
3. Do not put a bent pair of tweezers back among the good ones: with a bent tip it will not pick
   anything up, and you only find that out by trying.
4. If something is missing or broken, tell us.

## Access

The shelf is in the hackspace room, so a plan that includes hackspace access is required. No
induction is needed.
