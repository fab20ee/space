A couple of HP docks live on the peripherals shelf — for those who arrive with a full set of
habits: monitor, keyboard, mouse, charger. One USB-C cable into the laptop connects all of it at
once, and the laptop charges through the same cable.

Not everyone needs one: the monitor on the desk also connects directly, over HDMI or USB-C. The
dock is for when you want to assemble a complete workstation and plug it all in with one move.

## What connects to it

- A monitor — over DisplayPort or HDMI (cables are on the desks)
- Keyboard, mouse, flash drive — 4 USB-A ports
- A headset — combined audio jack
- Laptop power — through the same USB-C cable

## Access

Take one from the shelf, use it at any desk, put it back when you leave — the docks are shared,
like the mice and keyboards. Included in every plan.
