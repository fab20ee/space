Every seat in the coworking room is a motorised sit-stand desk. The height changes with a button
on the panel under the desktop, and the current value shows on the display in centimetres. Back
gone stiff — raise the desk and work standing for half an hour without breaking your flow.

## Why it matters

- The desk adjusts to you, not the other way around — both for the chair and for standing work
- Four memory presets: save your sitting and standing heights and return to them with one press
- The display shows the height in centimetres, so you can set the same position at any desk

## How to use it

1. Use the ▲/▼ arrows to raise or lower the desktop.
2. Save a comfortable height into one of the M1–M4 presets — then just press it.
3. Before raising the desk, check that cables and your mug are clear of the desktop's path.

## Access

The desks are in the coworking room and are included in every plan — day, week or month. Nothing
to book: come in, take a free desk, work.
