There is an HP monitor on every desk: spending the whole day in a single laptop screen is a
questionable pleasure. The stand adjusts for height and tilt, so the top of the screen sits at
eye level rather than wherever it happens to reach.

Connect with the HDMI cable that is already on the desk. If your laptop has no HDMI, there are
USB-C inputs — and a couple of HP docks on the peripherals shelf.

## Why it matters

- A second screen for your laptop — code on the left, documentation on the right
- A screen at eye level instead of looking down into a laptop all day
- Laptop shut in the bag — a monitor plus a mouse and keyboard from the shelf make the desk a full workstation

## Access

The monitors are on the coworking desks and are included in every plan. Please leave the cables
on the desk — the next person needs them right there.
