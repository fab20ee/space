A box of wireless mice and a stack of keyboards sit on a shelf in the coworking room — take
whatever you need to any desk. A touchpad is fine on the road, but over an eight-hour day at a
desk your hand will thank you for a real mouse. It is one of those small things people praise
here more often than we expected.

## How it works

1. Take a mouse or a keyboard from the shelf.
2. The USB receiver is in the compartment on the underside; plug it into your laptop or the dock.
3. When you leave, put the device and the receiver back on the shelf — the next person will thank you.

## Access

Included in every plan. If a battery dies, tell us or drop a message and we will swap it.
