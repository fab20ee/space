## Do I need a membership or an interview?

No. This is a commercial service: sign in with Smart-ID, pay for a plan, and the door opens.
There is nobody you need to arrange anything with.

## I don't have Smart-ID. What now?

Not a problem. [Sign up](/en/login) with your e-mail; we will sign the agreement remotely
or meet you at the hackspace.
