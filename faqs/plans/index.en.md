---

## Can I come for a single day?

Yes, there is a day plan. If you like it, top up more days or switch to a month —
what you already paid is not lost.

## Can I get access for free?

Sometimes — if you have a project that would be interesting to see in the workshop and no
budget for a plan. Write to clients@fab20.ee about what you are making. [More](/en/free-access).

## Can I give access as a gift?

Yes. A certificate for a day, a week or a month is sold in the [RainWalker store](https://store.rainwalker.ee/products/fab20-gift-card);
the recipient enters the code instead of paying.
