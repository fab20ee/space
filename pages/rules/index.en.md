**This is a short digest of the [Agreement](/en/membership-agreement)** — for quick reading, not
as a replacement. If anything differs, the text of the Agreement applies.

## Allowed

- Come at any time: the space runs 24/7, access within the zone of your plan
- Use any equipment of your zone; consumables within reason are included
- Coffee, tea, the kitchen, Wi-Fi — no limits
- Bring your own materials — at your own risk
- Ask for help; being sociable is optional

## Not allowed

- Letting other people or animals in: the door opens only for yourself
- Using equipment without studying its page on the site; where an induction is required, not without it
- Using faulty equipment, or repairing and modifying it yourself
- Smoking in any form (including e-cigarettes) and being under the influence
- Making more noise than the work requires; the coworking room has a quiet rule
- Taking FAB20 equipment or belongings out — except gear rented under a separate contract
- Living in the premises or throwing parties
- Entering the "Team only" areas

## Required

- Clean up after yourself: the workplace stays clean, dishes go into the dishwasher
- Report breakages right away: clients@fab20.ee or the form in the cabinet
- In case of fire or injury — 112 first, then tell us
- Damage is compensated by whoever caused it
