Some of the workshop's tools can go home or to a site with you: measurement instruments,
soldering gear — the things that are handy to have around for a couple of days but not worth
buying.

Everything happens on your phone: pick the period, pay by card, come and take it. There is no
staff on site, so the door opens for you at any hour, even if you have no access days.

## How it works

1. Sign in with Smart-ID and sign the agreement in the cabinet. It is one agreement for
   everything; there is no separate rental contract.
2. Open the tool's page, choose the number of days and pay by card. The period starts today.
3. Open the door from the cabinet, find the tool by the code on its sticker and take it. Mark
   "I took it" in the cabinet or by scanning the sticker's QR code.
4. Bring the tool back by the end of the last day and mark "I brought it back".

## Period, price, refunds

The price is per day and shown on the tool's page; there is no deposit. Rentals run from one to
fourteen days; they cannot be extended, but a new rental can follow if nobody has taken the tool
in between.

Until the tool is picked up, a rental can be cancelled by writing to clients@fab20.ee before the
end of the first day, with a full refund. Once the tool is picked up, the fee is not refunded.

## Responsibility

The tool comes back in the condition and completeness it was received in. Loss, damage and
non-return are on the renter: the cost of repair or replacement, and the same daily fee for each
day of delay. Details in clauses 5.11–5.15 of the agreement.
