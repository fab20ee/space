A small, quiet room in central Tallinn where you can work in peace, day or night. The hackspace
with its machines is behind the wall, and its noise does not reach here.

It runs around the clock and there is nobody you need to meet: sign the agreement with Smart-ID,
pay by card and open the door from your phone.

Everything you need for comfortable work is included — sit-stand desks, comfortable chairs,
monitors, coffee and tea.

## What's included

- A sit-stand desk: the height changes at the press of a button, so part of the day can be spent standing
- Comfortable chairs of several kinds
- Monitors with USB-C and HDMI, wireless mice, keyboards and a couple of HP docks
- Fast Wi-Fi
- A big shared table, a whiteboard for notes, printers and scanners
- Cleaning, so the place looks the same on Monday morning as it did on Friday
- A coffee machine: espresso, cappuccino, flat white, plus tea and light snacks
- A kitchen corner with a fridge for your own food and a microwave

## A place for you

There are no interviews, approvals or joining fees — this is a commercial service. Coming here
to work and never speaking to anyone is a perfectly normal way to use the place. And so is
sharing an idea and some inspiration with others — if that is what you want.

## We also have a hackspace

Behind the door is the hackspace: a laser cutter, 3D printers, soldering and measurement
equipment — you can use all of that too.
