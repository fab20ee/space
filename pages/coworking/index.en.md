A room to work in: quiet, with a desk you can actually think at. It sits next to the hackspace
but is a separate room, so machine noise stays where the machines are.

Access is round the clock and does not involve meeting anyone. You sign the membership agreement
with SmartID, pay by card, and the door opens from your phone — the whole thing takes about five
minutes, at any hour.

## What's included

- A desk, a chair that is meant for eight hours, and power at every seat
- Fast wired and wireless internet
- Coffee, tea and snacks
- A meeting room for calls and small meetings
- Kitchen with a fridge, microwave and dishwasher
- Cleaning, so the place looks the same on Monday morning as it did on Friday
- Printing and scanning

No separate charges for electricity, for the coffee machine, or for "membership" on top of the
plan. One price, everything in it.

## The quiet rule

Phone calls in the working area are kept quiet, and loud conversations move to the meeting room
or the kitchen. This is the one house rule that people actually notice, and it is the reason the
room works.

## What you don't have to do

You don't have to be approved by anyone, be interviewed, pay a joining fee, or become part of a
community. It is a service: you pay, you get a key. Coming here to work and never speaking to
anyone is a perfectly normal way to use the place.

## If you also want the workshop

The hackspace next door has the laser cutter, the 3D printers and the electronics bench. It is a
separate plan, because it is a noisier room with different rules — but the coworking area is
included in it.
