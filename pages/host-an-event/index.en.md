Want to run a workshop, a club meeting, a talk or a private event? There is a quiet room with a
screen and desks, and a workshop with equipment, in central Tallinn, at any hour. Free open
meetings we support; commercial events run on agreed terms. Write to us and we will settle the
date and the format.

## What there is

- Coworking room: desks and chairs for 12–16 people, a 65" screen, a kitchen with coffee and tea
- Workshop: laser cutter, 3D printers, soldering stations — for workshops with an induction
- Central address, around-the-clock access, parking nearby

## How to arrange it

Email or Telegram: what the event is, how many people, when, whether participants pay. We answer
within a day. For regular meetings we set up a club page on the site and let the host announce
the dates.
