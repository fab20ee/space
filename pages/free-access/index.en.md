Sometimes we give access for free. Not on proof of income and not by competition — to people
who have a project that would be interesting to see in the workshop and no budget for a plan.
Educational, open-source, research, odd — write to us about what you are making and we will
answer.

## What we ask in return

Tell about the project: a short page on the site once there is something to show, and, if you
like, a few words at one of the meetings. Everything else is the same as for everyone: house
rules, inductions on the machines, cleaning up after yourself.

## How it works

Write to [clients@fab20.ee](mailto:clients@fab20.ee): who you are, what the project is, how much
time and which equipment you need. If we agree, we send a promo code and the rest is the usual
path: sign in with SmartID, pick a plan, enter the code instead of paying, open the door from
your phone.
