Board game evenings in the quiet coworking room. No membership and no experience needed: games
are explained at the table, and the company is mixed.

## How it works

The host announces the date in the chat a few days ahead, comes to the coworking room and opens
the door for the participants. There are games on site, and you can bring your own. Coffee and
tea are in the kitchen, free.

## Who it is for

Anyone who wants to spend an evening at a table with people rather than a screen. Languages at
the table — Russian, Estonian, English, whatever works.
